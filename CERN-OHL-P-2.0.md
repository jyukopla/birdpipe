### Preamble

The CERN Open Hardware Licence (“OHL”) is a licence for the use,
copying, modification and distribution of hardware design documentation,
and the manufacture and distribution of products. The CERN-OHL-P variant
is a permissive licence that allows redistribution and use with minimal
conditions, similar in spirit to permissive software licences such as MIT.

### Definitions

“Documentation” means the design materials, schematics, HDL, PCB files,
mechanical drawings, bills of materials, and other materials necessary to
reproduce or modify the hardware.

“Product” means any device or physical implementation manufactured using
the Documentation.
